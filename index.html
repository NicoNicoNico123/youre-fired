    HOW TO PLAY 玩法

    🖥️ Desktop — first person
    • Click the screen once to lock the mouse, then move the mouse to look around
    • W A S D to walk around your workplace
    • Left-click an object to grab it — it floats in front of your hands
    • Flick the mouse and release (left-click) to hurl it!
    • Right-click or E = gentle place · Mouse wheel = reach closer / farther
    • R = 🧲 recall props you threw too far
    📱 Mobile / Touch — first person
    • Drag empty space to look around · WASD-less: everything is within reach
    • Touch an object to grab it — it floats in front of you
    • Flick and release to throw it!
    • ✋ PLACE button sets it down gently · 🧲 RECALL brings far props back
    📋 Customers & orders
    • Customers walk up and order food / documents — check the ORDER banner
    • Serve the right item for a tip... serve the wrong item (or throw it at them) for much more Rage
    • Your BOSS is always somewhere behind you. Turn around. He knows.
    😤 Goal
    • Make NPCs angry — throw stuff at them (head shots score more!)
    • Destroy & misuse workplace equipment, create chain reactions, discover secret combinations
    • Reach 100% RAGE as fast as possible. Any chaos counts — nothing is mandatory!
    • Repeating the same trick gives less Rage — mix it up for VARIETY BONUS!
